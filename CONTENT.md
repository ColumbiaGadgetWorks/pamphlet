What is in the pamphlet, what else could go in, and what to check before
printing a batch. Facts come from the CGW website (columbiagadgetworks.org).

## Other ideas (not included, space permitting)

- **City of Columbia / OCA logo.** Grant recipients often must show the funder's logo, not only a credit line. Check the grant agreement and get the official file from OCA.
- **Small map** of the building and lot, or a photo of the entrance door. Needs a real photo or a hand-drawn map.
- **Next few class dates and topics** as a dated insert or sticker, so the main pamphlet doesn't go out of date.
- **Member quote or two** about what they built or learned.
- **Scholarship memberships**, if and when the board brings them back (hidden on the website for now).
- **A "what will you make?" project strip**: cast pendants, laser-cut signs, repaired gadgets, using real member photos.
- **A one-page tour handout** variant for groups: a map of the zones in walking order.
- **Social handles** (Facebook, Bluesky) spelled out, if people ask for them.
