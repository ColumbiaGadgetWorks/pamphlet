Who we are

Columbia's community makerspace

A makerspace is a shared workshop. Think of a public library, but for tools. By pooling dues and donations, members share equipment few people could buy or fit at home, and share what they know about using it.

Columbia Gadget Works is a volunteer-run, member-supported 501(c)(3) nonprofit, in the same building on Grand Avenue since 2017. Our mission is an inclusive workshop where people of all skill levels can explore, learn, and build.

Thursdays, 6 to 8 pm · Free

What happens at Open Hack Night

  • Get a tour. A member shows you every zone in about ten minutes.
  • Use the basic tools. Hand tools, the electronics bench, and the 3D printers with a member's help.
  • See the big machines. The laser, CNC, mill, and lathe need training, but someone will gladly demo them.
  • Bring a project, or don't. A broken lamp, a half-built robot, a file to print, or nothing at all.

Built here

The Chamber of Robotic Aggression, a member-built combat robot arena. In 2020, members also designed the open-source Roll-to-Prone bed to help hospitals turn COVID-19 patients safely.

The shop

Equipment

Shared like a library, in zones run by volunteer leads. Members use anything they're trained on.

Laser cutter80 W CO₂. Cuts and engraves wood, acrylic, leather.
CNC routerShapeoko 4 XL. Signs, parts, and furniture joinery.
3D printersBambu Lab X1 Carbon and A1, multi-color.
Metal shopBridgeport mill, Clausing lathe, MIG/TIG/stick welding.
Metal castingTurn a 3D print into brass, bronze, or aluminum.
Electronics benchSoldering, hot-air rework, oscilloscope, parts.
WoodshopSawStop contractor table saw, plus a miter saw, band saw, planer, router table, drill press, and sanders.

The laser, CNC router, metal shop, casting, and table saw require training first. Tool guides and settings are on the wiki.

Learn something new

Free monthly classes

First Thursday of every month

A free public class every month from November 2026 through October 2027, starting November 5. Topics rotate through every zone in the shop. No membership or sign-up needed.

Recent classes include metal casting, soldering, machining on the mill and lathe, and a field trip to KOPN community radio. Members teach every class.
